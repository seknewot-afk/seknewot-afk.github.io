Cigarette Counter: Pause

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Cigarette Counter: Pause handles information. The app is published under the developer name SekneWot. The data controller is Sekne Wot, who can be reached at the contact address below.

Your journal stays on your device

Pause does not require an account. The app stores your cigarette entries, including dates, times and product details, along with your goals, cost settings, preferences and progress on your device. Pause uses this information to display your journal, calculate your statistics and update your companion.

Pause does not send your journal, cigarette counts or goals to the developer or to Google AdMob. The app uses your journal on your device to provide the tracking features you request.

Advertising and privacy choices

Pause uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and occasional full-screen ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as your IP address, interactions with the app or ads, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate your general location.

Pause uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising choices. Where consent is required for a particular advertising use, that use is subject to your consent. You can review or change your choices in Settings → Privacy options when that option is available. For information about Google’s processing, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups you choose to export

You can export a backup and restore it later. A backup may contain your journal and settings. If you save or share a backup through another service, that service handles the file under its own privacy policy. You choose where the backup is stored and who can access it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your journal stays on the device until you delete it or remove the app’s local data. To erase it from Pause, open Settings and choose Delete all data. Delete any exported backups separately from the locations where you saved them.

Support and external links

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond to your request. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You do not need to send your full journal to ask for help. If you follow a link to an external website, that website’s privacy policy applies.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of personal information, to object to certain uses, and to withdraw consent. Contact us for requests about information we handle. For information handled by Google, see the controls and contacts in Google’s Privacy Policy. If EU data protection law applies to you, you can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
